## Proyek dan checkpoints

Pure trajectory/solver, simple world/collision, then animation state and renderer.

Checkpoints: Variable display cadence, tunneling, contact, pause/resume, repeated state changes.

## Batas verifikasi

Contact engine dan game renderer tidak diimplementasikan penuh pada v0.1.
